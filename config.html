Probe Settings

Distances

A longer measurement distance gives a more accurate angle. Max probe distance limits how far the probe travels toward the edge before giving up.

Feed Rates

Probe feed is the speed toward the edge. Travel feed is used to retract and to move between the two probe points; that move is a G38.3 probe-away so an unexpected contact stops it.
